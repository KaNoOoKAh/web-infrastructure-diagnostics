- Duplicate entries

## 2. Time Zone and DST Boundary Effects

Daylight Saving Time transitions can produce known anomalies including duplicate execution windows, skipped operations, ambiguous timestamps, and scheduling conflicts. These effects are well-documented in software systems. 
### Indicators
- Recurring task irregularities
- Calendar discrepancies
- Notification timing changes
- Scheduling inconsistencies

### Classification
**Priority:** High during time transitions
