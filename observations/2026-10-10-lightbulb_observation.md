#### Observation Details

During the past week, two LED light bulbs configured to a color temperature setting of 6500K have exhibited intermittent flickering behavior. The affected bulbs periodically turn off completely and subsequently restore illumination without user intervention.
The observed behavior has been consistent across both affected bulbs operating at the 6500K setting. No similar issues have been observed when the same type of bulbs are operated at 2700K or when configured to the light blue color mode.

#### Conditions Observed

Flickering occurs only while bulbs are set to 6500K.
Bulbs may extinguish completely before returning to normal operation.
Other color settings appear stable.
The condition has persisted for approximately one week.

#### Preliminary Assessment

The issue appears to be associated with the bulbs' operation at the 6500K color temperature rather than a general power interruption. Possible contributing factors include:
Internal LED driver instability when operating specific white LED channels.
Firmware or controller issues affecting the 6500K setting.
Degradation of LED components used primarily for the 6500K output.
Voltage regulation issues that become apparent only under the power demand profile of the daylight-white setting.

#### Recommended Follow-Up Actions

Verify whether both bulbs are connected to the same circuit or controller.
Observe if flickering frequency increases over time.
Test replacement bulbs at 6500K under the same conditions.
Check for available firmware updates if the bulbs are smart-enabled.
Monitor household voltage stability during occurrences.

#### Summary

Two LED bulbs have demonstrated intermittent flickering and temporary shutdowns over the past week when configured to 6500K daylight white. The same bulb model remains stable when operated at 2700K or light blue, suggesting the anomaly may be linked to the specific color temperature mode rather than the electrical supply as a whole.
